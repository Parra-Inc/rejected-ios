# Rejected — X / Twitter Playbook

> The viral primitive lives here. The share screen is built for the X aspect ratio. The reframe-as-flex format is native to X. Segments 1, 3, and 5 of our audience are on this platform daily.

---

## 1. Why X is rank #1

| Reason | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Format-native** | The "screenshot + one-line caption" format is the dominant viral primitive on X. Our share image is purpose-built for it. |
| **Audience density** | Indie hackers (seg 1), founders pitching investors (seg 3), and rejection-therapy challengers (seg 5) all live here daily. |
| **Reply-bait surface** | "How many App Store rejections this year?" gets 50–500 replies because the answer is socially performative. X is built for this. |
| **Founder voice = brand voice** | The brand is "lowercase Marc Lou meets defiant Jia Jiang." This voice doesn't survive on LinkedIn or TikTok in the same way. |
| **Cheapest CAC** | A single banger tweet (>50K imps) is functionally a free $500 ad. We expect 2–4 per quarter. |

---

## 2. Account architecture

We run **two accounts**:

1. **@rejectedapp** — product handle. Logo avatar (gold trophy on ink). Bio: `Collect rejections like trophies. iOS app, free. Built by @[founder] at @parra_io.` Pinned tweet: the launch thread.
2. **Founder personal account** — primary voice. The founder posts as themselves, mentions the app sparingly (1 in 7 posts is product-related), and shares rejection content from their own life. This is where the reach lives.

Ratio: founder account does 80% of the heavy lifting. Product account replies, retweets, and runs official "Rejection Week" updates.

---

## 3. Cadence

| Cadence | Founder account | @rejectedapp |
|---|---|---|
| **Daily** | 3 posts/day (1 build update + 1 rejection screenshot + 1 reply-storm in a target thread) | 1 quote-RT of the best community rejection post |
| **Weekly** | 1 longer thread (5–9 tweets, narrative) | 1 #RejectionWeek-style themed post |
| **Monthly** | 1 banger attempt (data post, list post, vulnerable post) | "State of rejection this month" stat drop |

Reply-storm target threads daily — pick one founder's tweet (Marc Lou, Levels, Theo) and write a substantive reply within 30 min of post time. Founder replies are the single highest-ROI growth tactic for accounts under 10K followers.

## 4. The seven post archetypes

Every founder post we draft should fit one of these. If it doesn't, it's probably not on-brand.

### A1. The screenshot reply-bait
> Apple rejected my app for the 11th time.
>
> Guideline 4.3 — "design spam" — on an app that didn't exist before I built it.
>
> Logged in Rejected. Streak: 11.
>
> [screenshot of stats card]

**When to use:** any actual rejection that happens. App Review, investor email, polite no from a podcast host, anything. The screenshot is the receipt.

### A2. The flex
> 0 yeses this week
>
> 14 no's logged
>
> Best week of the year.

**When to use:** Sunday or Monday morning. Sets the tone for the week.

### A3. The Marc Lou format (ask the crowd)
> indie hackers: how many App Store rejections this year?
>
> me: 17 🥲
>
> reply with yours, I'll add the highest to the demo data of my app

**When to use:** once every 10–14 days. Burns out if used more often. Goal is 100+ replies.

### A4. The list post (high-RT)
> Things I tracked in 2026:
> – 87 investor passes
> – 64 job rejections
> – 11 Apple rejections
> – 3 YC rejections
> – 1 polite no from my mom on a co-sign
>
> All of it in one place: rejected.app

**When to use:** monthly. The numbers must be real or believably real.

### A5. The Jia Jiang nod (philosophy)
> Jia Jiang spent 100 days asking for rejection and gave a 10M-view TED talk about it.
>
> Most of us get 100 rejections without trying.
>
> The least we can do is log them.

**When to use:** when a high-profile rejection happens in the news (mass layoffs, big YC rejection thread, viral lit mag rejection story). Ride the wave with a philosophical anchor.

### A6. The Levels.io format (one-liner)
> 97% of what I ship flops.
>
> 100% of what I ship gets logged.

**When to use:** whenever you're stuck for ideas. The two-line format ships in 2 minutes and lands consistently.

### A7. The build-in-public daily
> Day 32 building Rejected (open source SwiftUI app for tracking rejections):
>
> Today: tip jar polish. 99¢ ☕️ / $2.99 🍕 / $9.99 🎉
> Open rate on share screen: 41%.
> Tips received yesterday: 4.
>
> Building in public.

**When to use:** 2–3x/week during launch. The audience rewards transparency, especially with revenue numbers (even small ones — they're more relatable).

## 7. Hashtag use

Sparingly. X has deprioritized hashtags in the algorithm; their use today is *signaling tribe membership* more than discovery.

- `#RejectionWeek` — own this hashtag during launch and re-run it annually.
- `#buildinpublic` — once or twice a week max on build-update posts.
- `#rejectiontherapy` — for Jia-Jiang-adjacent content.
- Skip everything else.

---

## 8. Quote-RT economy

Quoting > replying for our growth. Two QTs per day from the founder account:

1. **One QT of a community rejection post** — adds a one-line reframe + link to app: `"this is exactly the kind of receipt that compounds. logged: rejected.app"`
2. **One QT of an industry story** (layoff news, App Store policy change, YC announcement) — adds a brand-on-brand observation: `"YC accepted 0.6% this cycle. That's 39,800 founders with a receipt. log them: rejected.app"`

Avoid: QT-dunking on bad takes. Off-brand and unfounder-like.

## 9. DM playbook

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Someone tweets a rejection screenshot organically | DM within 1 hour: "Saw your tweet — built an app for exactly this. Free, no catch. Would love your feedback: rejected.app" |
| Mid-tier creator (5K–50K followers) mentions Jia Jiang / rejection therapy | DM: "saw your post on [X]. I built a free app for the 100-day challenge — would you take a look and tell me if it's the right fit for your audience?" |
| Heavy followee (100K+) tweets about layoffs | Reply publicly only. Do not DM cold. They get hundreds. |
| Reply to your post is from someone with audience | DM: "thanks for the reply. would you want a TestFlight invite to try the next build before it ships?" |

Daily DM budget: 10–15. Anything more reads as spam.

## 10. Measurement

We don't have great X-to-install attribution. Use these proxies:

| Metric | Source | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Followers added/week (founder account) | X analytics | +100/week launch, +200/week steady |
| Followers added/week (@rejectedapp) | X analytics | +20/week |
| Avg impressions/post | X analytics | 2K → 8K → 25K |
| Bangers per quarter (>50K imps) | Manual tracking | 2–4 |
| Inbound DMs/week | Inbox | 5 → 20 |
| `rejected.app` UTM visits from `x.com` referrer | GA4 | 200/wk → 2K/wk |

If we end 60 days with no banger tweet, the voice or format is off. Audit and iterate.

## 11. Anti-patterns

- **Don't reply with just emojis.** Always add a sentence with substance.
- **Don't be the founder who only posts about their product.** 1-in-7 ratio. The other 6 are about the topic (rejection, building, the layoff cycle).
- **Don't fight in QTs.** If a take is bad, ignore it. Brand voice is defiant about *the work*, not defensive about *the product*.
- **Don't apologize in copy.** "Sorry to self-promote but..." reads as weakness. We are not sorry.
- **Don't use threads for marketing pitches.** Threads should narrate a story, list lessons, or break down data. The pitch is the last tweet of the thread, not the first.
- **Don't tweet from the @rejectedapp handle in the founder's voice.** Keep the brand handle to RTs, QTs, official #RejectionWeek updates, and one milestone post per week.
- **Don't buy followers, do follow-trains, or use engagement pods.** The algorithm will punish you, and the audience will smell it.

## 12. Tools & ops

- **Drafting:** Typefully or in-app drafts (whichever the founder already uses). All seven archetypes saved as templates.
- **Scheduling:** Light scheduling only. Most posts go out live to allow real-time reply triage. Schedule the daily 7am post.
- **Analytics:** X native analytics (free); SocialBlade for follower trend.
- **DM tracking:** Notion table with columns `handle | first DM date | response | result`. Update weekly.
- **Screenshot prep:** Keep a folder of 20 pre-cropped app screenshots so a tweet ships in <60 seconds.
