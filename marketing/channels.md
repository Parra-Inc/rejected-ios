# Rejected — Channel Mix

> Free app. No ad budget required for product-market fit. Every dollar of paid is optional and gated on the (future) Pro tier shipping. Organic and earned are the entire engine.

---

## 1. Channel philosophy

**The growth loop is the share screen.** Every saved rejection in the app produces a shareable artifact. That artifact is the ad unit. Our job is to make it more frictionless to share, route it to channels where our segments live, and never get in the way of the user posting it themselves.

That means:
- We invest where the artifact spreads (X, LinkedIn, TikTok, IH, HN, Reddit).
- We deprioritize where the artifact dies (paid social display, podcast ads, banner ads).
- We invest in **content** that backs up the brand and **community** that hosts our audience.

## 3. Budget split (assuming any paid spend, post-Pro launch)

Today: **$0/mo paid.** Founder time is the budget.

After Pro tier ships (post 10K MAU):

| Channel | Share | Monthly $ at $500/mo | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apple Search Ads | 70% | $350 | Tier 4 → Tier 2 only after Pro ships |
| Newsletter sponsorships (1/mo, rotating) | 20% | $100 | Sub Club, Bootstrapped Founder, IH digest |
| Product Hunt scheduled launch boosts | 5% | $25 | Optional |
| Reserve | 5% | $25 | Test budget |

Caps:
- Never spend more than 50% of **trailing 90-day tip + Pro revenue** on paid.
- Kill any ad set with $50+ spend and 0 installs.
- Re-evaluate quarterly.

## 7. Kill criteria per channel

| Channel | Kill if... | What to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| X | <500 followers added by Day 30 across 80+ posts | Audit voice — likely too earnest or too snarky. Pivot toward more numerical/screenshot posts. |
| LinkedIn | Zero posts above 50K impressions by Day 60 | Hire a ghostwriter for one month to test format vs voice issue. |
| Communities | Show HN doesn't make front page (≥150 upvotes) | Wait 90 days, rewrite with deeper personal story, resubmit. |
| Content marketing | Rejection Wall has <20 submissions by Day 60 | Reduce ambition — switch to a quarterly "State of App Review Rejection" post instead of weekly cadence. |
| Product launches | PH finishes outside top 10 launch day | Don't re-launch. Skip PH for v2; lean into Show HN re-runs. |
| Newsletter swaps | <2 swaps confirmed by Day 60 | Switch from cold pitches to paid sponsorships ($500/issue rotating). |
| TikTok | <500 followers in 60 days across 24 videos | Stop. Single-creator partnership (one Jia Jiang or Mel Robbins mention) will outperform 6 months of solo content. |
| Apple Search Ads | CPI > $15 with $300 spend, no Pro conversions | Pause until Pro tier ships and LTV is known. |

## 8. Channels we are deliberately not pursuing

| Channel | Why not |
|---|---|
| Instagram feed (non-Reels) | The single-image feed format does not fit the share screen. Stories/Reels do. |
| Facebook (any surface) | Audience age skew is wrong; segments 1/3/5 are not there. |
| Snapchat | Audience too young; rejection-therapy isn't a teen behavior. |
| Pinterest | No fit. |
| Google Search Ads | iOS-only product + free + low-volume search terms = poor unit economics. |
| Discord ads | No mature ad product. Engage in Discord servers (RevenueCat, iOS Dev Happy Hour, Indie Apps Catalyst) organically instead. |
| Influencer marketplaces (Aspire, Grin) | Our creator targets are people we DM directly. Marketplace creators won't have audience fit. |
| Outbound PR (TechCrunch / Verge pitches) | Almost zero pickup rate for a free utility. Pitch indie founder podcasts instead. |
| Conferences / events | Too expensive per touch. Sponsor only if we get a co-marketing deal (e.g., MicroConf cross-promo). |
| Email lead-gen tools (Lemlist, Apollo) | DMing real people on real platforms beats cold email by 10x for this audience. |

## 9. The 80/20

If we can only do four things:

1. **Show HN at launch.** One post, one day, can deliver 5K installs and seed every other channel for the year.
2. **Rejection Week social event.** Names a behavior people already do. Compounds across every channel in one week.
3. **One LinkedIn long-form post per week.** The data post (Friday). Cheapest unlock for segment 2.
4. **The Rejection Wall web asset.** Compounds for years. SEO + community asset + tweet-bait per update.

Everything else is amplification.
