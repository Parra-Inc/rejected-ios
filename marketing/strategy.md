# Rejected — Marketing Strategy

> Collect rejections like trophies. The growth loop is the share screen — every saved rejection becomes a reason to brag in public.

The companion long-form playbook with cadences, KPIs, and metrics lives in [plan.md](plan.md). This document is the strategic overview — read this first, then dive into the per-channel deep dives.

---

## 1. The opportunity in one paragraph

There is no first-class iOS app for collecting rejections. The audience already shares rejections for free engagement (LinkedIn layoff posts, "Apple rejected my app again" tweets, Jia Jiang's #rejectiontherapy with 100M+ TikTok views, Pieter Levels publicly saying "97% of what I ship flops"). The behavior pre-exists; we are giving it a home, a hashtag, and a stats card. The marketing angle and the product angle are the same thing: **rejection reframed as data, evidence of effort, and a streak you can be proud of.**

---

## 2. Why now (2026)

1. **The "1,000 rejections" challenge** crossed mainstream in Jan 2026 (CNBC feature on Gabriella Carr's TikTok). Jia Jiang's TED talk passed 10.5M views in 39 languages.
2. **Tech layoffs:** layoffs.fyi shows 138,837+ tech workers laid off in 2026 alone. Median laid-off engineer reports 67+ rejections (our LinkedIn data post).
3. **YC rejection as content:** S25 acceptance hit 0.6% — 39,800+ rejections per cycle, openly posted on X for likes.
4. **Indie hackers brag about flop count.** The flex is the failure rate. The format already works; we ship the app for it.

---

## 3. The narrative leverage (this is the whole game)

The marketing angle **is the product**. There's no gap between message and tool:

- **The product is shareable.** Logging a rejection ends in the share screen. The share image becomes the tweet / LinkedIn post / TikTok. That post drives installs. That install creates more rejections to share. It is a closed loop and the loop is the brand.
- **The reframe is the wedge.** "Rejection is data, not a verdict" is a thesis people already want to believe. We are giving them an artifact (a stats card) that makes the belief tangible.
- **The audience is already producing the content.** We are not creating new behavior. We are claiming the hashtag for a behavior people already do for free.

This is why we lead with organic and earned, not paid. Paid would chase an audience that already comes to us if we name what they're doing.

---

## 4. Audience (3-sentence summary — see [segments.md](segments.md))

Five overlapping tribes, all defined by *deliberate effort that produces rejection at scale*: indie hackers shipping fast (App Store rejections), job seekers in the layoff wave (LinkedIn rejections), founders pitching investors (47 polite passes), writers submitting work (lit mag form letters), and rejection-therapy challengers (Jia Jiang readers running 100-day challenges). All five already post rejection content publicly for engagement. The unifying psychographic is **"I'd rather collect no's than not try at all."**

---

## 5. Positioning (one-liner)

> **Rejected — Collect rejections like trophies.**

Every "no" is proof you tried. Job rejections, App Store rejections, YC rejections, life rejections — track them, share them, get back to work.

Full message house and against-statements in [positioning.md](positioning.md).

---

## 6. Pricing posture

Free forever. Tip jar at $0.99 / $2.99 / $9.99 (consumable IAPs, already shipped). No subscription, no required login. The premise of the app is generosity to people in a low-resource moment (laid off, between rounds, getting rejected); charging would betray it.

A future $2.99/yr Pro tier is on the table after 10K MAU (CSV export, widgets, custom icons, founder/job mode field templates), but core stays free forever. See plan.md §6 for full rationale.

## 8. The one-week launch sequence

This is the bet. Everything else is a multiplier on this week.

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| **-7 to -1** | Seed seven daily template posts ourselves (one per segment) to demonstrate the format. |
| **Mon (D0)** | Launch post: "It's #RejectionWeek. Share 1 rejection a day for 7 days. Public ones get the most love." Submit Show HN 8:00 AM PT. |
| **Tue–Sat** | Quote-RT the top 5 posts daily. Cross-post to LinkedIn + IH + Reddit (rotate subs). Product Hunt launch Tuesday-Thursday. |
| **Sun** | Announce $500 winner. Ship v1.0.1 with their rejection seeded in demo data ("inspired by @username"). |

Targets for the week: 500 #RejectionWeek posts across X+LinkedIn, 10K App Store impressions, 1K installs, 1 newsletter or podcast pickup.

## 10. What we are not (anti-positioning)

- Not a journaling app. Rejections only.
- Not a productivity app. No tasks, no goals.
- Not a depression journal. The frame is defiant-optimistic, not therapeutic.
- Not a SaaS. No cloud login, no subscription, no AI summarizer.
- Not a therapist. Vibes and stats.

If you find yourself writing marketing that sounds like therapy, journaling, or self-help — stop. We are the gym log of doing hard things, not the diary of feeling bad about them.
