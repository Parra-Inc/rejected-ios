# Rejected — Positioning

> The marketing angle and the product angle are the same thing: rejection reframed as evidence of effort. This document is the message house — every channel pulls from here.

---

## 1. One-liner

**Rejected — Collect rejections like trophies.**

## 2. Elevator pitch (60 sec)

> Rejected is a small iOS app that turns the worst part of doing hard things into a streak you're proud of. Log a rejection in 5 seconds — pick a category (Job, Investor, YC, Apple Review, lit mag, life), give it a title, drop a note. Get a stats card, a share screen, and a little dose of "back to work" energy. Free forever. Tip the developer if it made you smile.

## 3. Three taglines (rotate across surfaces)

1. **"Get told no on the record."** Every rejection logged is proof you tried.
2. **"The trophy case for the no's."** Job rejections, App Store rejections, YC rejections, life rejections.
3. **"Rejection is data. Track it."** Then go get one more.

## 4. Three audience-specific subtitles

- **For builders:** "The trophy case for App Store rejections, investor passes, and PH #4 finishes."
- **For job seekers:** "Track every 'no.' You're closer to the yes."
- **For challengers:** "The official tracker for your 100-day rejection challenge."

### Pillar 1 — Effort, not failure

The core reframe. Every channel post should sound like it could come from someone who genuinely believes this — because we do.

- "Receipts compound. Aspirations evaporate."
- "Applied is an aspiration. Rejected is a receipt."
- "97% of what I ship flops. 100% of what I ship gets logged."
- "The rejection volume is a feature of effort, not a bug of you."

### Pillar 2 — 5-second logging

The product proof. Differentiates from every "track your job applications" Notion template or Google Sheet.

- "Add a category. Title. Note. Done."
- "No account. No subscription. Nothing leaves your phone."
- "Native iOS. Built for thumbs in line at the coffee shop after the rejection email lands."

### Pillar 3 — Earned-share moment

The growth loop. The reason we are an app and not a journal — the share screen is the artifact people want to post.

- The 🤠 share image with "turn that frown upside down"
- Stats card with total / last / top category
- One-tap to X, LinkedIn, iMessage, Threads

## 6. Against statements (this is NOT...)

Use these to course-correct anyone (us included) drifting toward the wrong tone.

| This is NOT | Because |
|---|---|
| A depression journal | The frame is defiant-optimistic. Tracking rejection because you're going for things, not because you feel bad. |
| A productivity app | No tasks, no goals, no Notion-style bloat. We do one thing. |
| A therapy app | We are vibes and stats. We do not coach. We do not interpret. |
| A SaaS | No cloud login required. No subscription. No AI summarizer. No team plans. |
| A submissions manager (Duotrope/Submittable) | Those are workflow tools for *active submissions*. We track the *outcome*. Different verb. |
| A CRM for fundraising | We don't replace Visible.vc or Airtable. We sit next to them and absorb the emotional load. |
| A career coach | Bonnie Dilber will tell you what to fix. We will tell you you're still in the fight. |
| Self-deprecating | "I'm such a loser, look at all my rejections" is the wrong note. The note is "look at all this work I shipped." |

If a piece of copy reads more like a Calm app push notification than a Marc Lou tweet, rewrite it.

## 7. Voice & tone

**Slightly defiant. Optimistic without being saccharine. Cool with cursing if it lands.**

The voice is the friend who texts "lol Apple rejected me again, gonna be a great week" — not the friend who says "ugh I'm so over this." Both are valid emotional responses. We are unambiguously the first one.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Best week of the year." (after 0 yeses) | "Hang in there — better days ahead." |
| "Logged. Back to work." | "It's okay to feel disappointed." |
| "Your 47th investor no is the same as your 1st. Both are data." | "Investors don't define your worth." |
| "Rejection is data. Track it." | "Embrace your rejection journey." |
| Lowercase, terse, no exclamation points | Corporate-blog cadence |
| Emoji used as data ("🤠 47" "🍎 11") | Emoji as decoration ("🌟✨ amazing! ✨🌟") |
| Specific numbers ("$3 from tips today") | Vague aspirations ("growing the community") |

### Words we use
log, receipt, trophy case, streak, no, "on the record," ship, "back to work," collected, data, evidence, proof, "the grind," reframe

### Words we avoid
journey, embrace, healing, processing, wellness, mindfulness, resilience-as-a-noun, "you've got this," "warrior," "tribe" (use "crowd" or "audience"), "manifest"

## 8. Competitor positioning

| Alternative | Their pitch | Our wedge |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Notes / Google Sheets | Free and already installed | Native UI, stats, shareable artifact, category presets |
| Duotrope ($60/yr) | Web tracker for writer submissions | Native iOS, free, broader categories, share-as-trophy framing |
| The Submission Grinder | Free web writer tracker | Native iOS, social/share moment, broader audience |
| Notion templates | Customizable everything | Built for one task, in your pocket, share screen |
| Airtable for fundraising | Pipeline tracker | We don't replace it — we sit next to it for the emotional log |
| TikTok captions / camera roll | Free, already where rejection-therapy people live | Searchable, persistent, gives you a stats card to flex |

We do not have direct competition. The most honest competitive frame is **"the alternative to keeping rejection in your head."**
